Gauley Flush

Privacy

Gauley Flush is a scorekeeper for friendly card games. It never holds or moves real money.

What we store. The display name you type, your table's bets and totals, and a random seat token kept in your browser. If you choose "Continue with Google", we read only your name, email-verified identifier and profile picture from Google to recognise your seat. Nothing else from your Google account is accessed.

Line feedback. If you tap thumbs up or down on a witty line (and optionally add a note), we store the line id, your vote, your note, and a random device id kept in your browser. No name, no account.

How long. A table is deleted automatically after 24 hours of inactivity.

Sharing. We do not sell or share your data. Other players at your table can see your name and totals.
